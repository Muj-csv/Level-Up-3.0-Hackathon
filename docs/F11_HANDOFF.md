# F11 release readiness — handoff for Stages C, D, E

Stages A and B are finished on `Taympers/f11-release-readiness` (commit 8233166). This branch,
`Taympers/f11-stage-c-wip`, adds the work started for C, D and E on top of it. It is **work in progress**:
built and partly checked, not verified. Compare with `git diff Taympers/f11-release-readiness`.

## Already built (needs finishing and verifying)

### C. Graphics presets, performance, map readability
- **2D/3D choice removed:** Grapiko row gone from the pause menu, `g3d` gone from PREFS. Migration: an old `{g3d:false}` with no saved `gq` becomes `gq:'low'`.
  - All `PREFS.g3d` gates removed: `use3D`, `beginGame`, `update`, `titleLayout`, `heroInit`, `splashMenuScene`, and `galBuild` (the A+B commit gates it).
- **`maps/living.js` (root fix for grainy, misaligned grass on islands):** a per-island `uLW` matrix in the chain, water and grass-shell shaders, and a grass far-fade scaled by it.
  - Measured on Mataas at the same view: 780 → 429 draw calls, 475k → 299k triangles.
- **Palms kept off the board:** `crownGap` and `palmOffBoard` in `maps/islands/aplaya.js` and `palayan.js`. No palms dropped.
- **Board readability:**
  - kids sharing a space spread apart (`slotOffset`: 2 kids at ±22; 3–4 on an ellipse 30×17)
  - map kids drawn at scale 1.3
  - dice drawn above the kid's head
  - landscape tip box moved to the lower left
- **`B3.census()`** (debug only): visible meshes and shadow casters by owner, and calls/triangles with and without the shadow pass.
- **Tests:**
  - `tests/3d.mjs`: Kalidad checks replace the Grapiko checks.
  - `tests/quality.mjs`: migration checks, plus a wait for the first save (fixes a race).
  - `tests/four.mjs`: waits for the seat to return to local (fixes a race).

### D. Economy
- **Rides reachable and worth it:**
  - The stop moved early in the loop: Plaza M12→M68, Palengke M15→M65, Palayan M14→M66 (8 piso); Aplaya bangka 12→40 (6 piso).
  - A kid can ride when stopping on the stop or passing it; the move ends at the far stop, and a korona there can be bought.
  - Bot rule is in `ride()`.
- **Korona placement:** spots are chosen in reach of Simula first, then past the ride's far stop (`resetKorona`, `koronaInPlay`). `moveKorona` prefers spots someone can reach.
- **Fewer free piso spaces:**
  - A share of piso spaces became rotating island types: plaza .5, palengke .34, aplaya .4, palayan .4.
  - Shops are now 5–7 per island; piso spaces 28–33.
  - `maps/boards.js` is regenerated by the new `tools/boards-export.mjs` (`--check` verifies).
- **Ride text:** the guide and SPACE_TIP texts state the new ride rules.
- **Debug seed:** `startLocal({..., seed})` (debug only) for seeded bot simulations.

### E. Docs
README, `tests/README.md` and `docs/F10_PERFORMANCE.md` have been updated for Kalidad-only and the new ride rules.

## Measurements so far

- **Baseline:** 30 seeded bot games per island, Sakto bots, 5 rounds.
  - Korona bought per game: 2.0–3.0
  - Bonus korona share of the total: 53–63%
  - Rides per game: ~0
  - Piso-space landings: 44–61%
- **After the D changes:** quick 6-game check per island only.
  - Korona bought: 4.0–5.0
  - Bonus share: 39–48%
  - Rides per game: 0.5–1.7
  - Piso landings: 26–32%
  - No stuck games
- **Not done yet:**
  - the full 30-game "after" run
  - the "after" draw-call census on all presets
  - menu sweeps at 360×740, 390×844, 844×390 and desktop
  - re-rendering `maps/world` pictures (palms moved slightly)
  - real-device frame rates: never measured
